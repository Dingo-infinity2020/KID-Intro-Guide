## 6. v0.2 content added beyond v0.1

This milestone contains, in addition to the v0.1 fabrication chapters:

- a stable fabrication/cooldown data contract;
- template-compatible synthetic data generation;
- a conservative measured-data adapter keyed by `resonator_id`;
- explicit raw-vs-derived separation;
- provenance requirements for model sensitivities;
- collision audit using loaded linewidth and an explicit margin;
- `next_design.csv` as a review proposal rather than an automatic GDS rewrite;
- four standard diagnostic views: wafer `Rsq`, frequency error, predictor-vs-measurement, residual map;
- dependency-free SVG/HTML diagnostic generation;
- cross-batch repeatability comparison without a fake universal PASS threshold;
- a first-real-batch command-line runbook;
- a full textbook chapter turning the first real wafer/cooldown into an auditable feedback workflow.

## 7. Remaining non-blocking work

The milestone is technically and visually usable, but v0.2 remains a development branch rather than a published release. Remaining work includes:

- one small editorial cleanup in Chapter 10 where an older sentence still describes plotting as future work even though plotting is now implemented;
- replacing synthetic/example sensitivities with platform-grounded values only after real fabrication training or measured data exist;
- recording provenance and uncertainty for any model later promoted from a working hypothesis to a reusable process model.

None of these invalidate the current data contract, runnable examples, or 70-page PDF QA result.
